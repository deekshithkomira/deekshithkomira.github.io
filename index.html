Most organisations are not short of data. They are short of a version of it anyone trusts.

It sits in four systems that disagree, in a spreadsheet someone maintains by hand, and in the head of the one person who is on holiday.

So the reporting takes a week, the answer arrives after the decision, and nobody can say where the number came from.

That is the gap I work in — between the raw source and the moment someone acts on it.

About

A bit
about me.

Since 2022 I've worked across data quality, analytics and AI: at Deloitte, in pharma at Johnson & Johnson, at an Oracle partner, in an AI research lab, and in a Tufts classroom.

Most teams split that work between engineering, reporting and data science. I've worked on all three sides, so I don't lose things in the handoffs, which is usually where projects quietly die.

The part I actually enjoy is the messy bit: working out which system is telling the truth, getting the data somewhere I'd trust, then explaining the answer clearly enough that someone can act on it without having to take my word for it.

I'm based in Boston and open to data analyst, data quality and BI roles, on-site or hybrid, and happy to relocate anywhere in the U.S. Email is the quickest way to reach me: komira.dee@gmail.com
